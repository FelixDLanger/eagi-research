# Hand-check of the IT-related material-weakness indicator

*Validation step specified in advance in the findings register and the research protocol ("read the
flagged filings before the association carries any argument"). Carried out 20–21 October 2026 on
the public filings at SEC EDGAR. Reported in the EAGI working paper, Section 4.6 and Appendix C.23.*

**Object.** The 21 firm-years flagged `mw_it_aff = 1` in `mandatory_signals.csv` (eight firms). The
indicator is the extraction rule reproduced in Appendix A of the paper: an occurrence of "material
weakness" counts as IT-related when an IT cue occurs within 600 characters, and as affirmed when an
affirmation cue occurs within 220 characters and no negation cue does.

**Question asked of each filing.** Does the annual report disclose an IT-related material weakness
that existed at the flagged fiscal year-end, or one remediated during that year? Or is the flagged
language something else: a weakness remediated in an earlier year, hypothetical risk-factor
language, or a weakness that is not IT-related?

**Reading limit.** The reading tool returns roughly the first 100,000–130,000 characters of each
filing — the cover, Item 1 and most of Item 1A. Item 9A and late risk factors are beyond it. A
firm-year is classified only when the passage that can trigger the rule was found in the readable
text; otherwise it is recorded as unresolved, with what the readable text shows. Nothing below is
inferred from a neighbouring year.

## Result

| Category | Firm-years | Count |
|---|---|---|
| IT weakness existing at the flagged year-end | Microchip FY2019 · HP FY2023 | 2 |
| IT weakness remediated during the flagged year | Costco FY2019 · Microchip FY2020 | 2 |
| Weakness remediated in an earlier year, still described | Microchip FY2021 · Microchip FY2022 | 2 |
| Hypothetical risk-factor language only | NVIDIA FY2022 | 1 |
| Weakness exists but is not IT-related | Kraft Heinz FY2019 | 1 |
| Unresolved — trigger beyond the readable text | 13 firm-years (below) | 13 |

Of the eight resolved firm-years, four disclose an IT-related weakness of the flagged year (existing at
its end or remediated during it), and two of those four existed at the flagged year-end. The four genuine events come from three firms. The indicator
therefore does not measure "an IT control failure in the prior year": it admits remediation years,
historical mentions and hypothetical language as readily as current weaknesses.

## Two findings about the comparison flag

The indicator's non-convergence with the ICFR-not-effective flag (r = −0.006) is partly a failure of
the comparison flag. That flag matches one phrasing, "internal control over financial reporting
was/were not effective". It misses Kraft Heinz FY2019, which states that the company "did not
maintain effective internal control over financial reporting", and HP FY2023, whose year-end
weakness precludes an effective conclusion under 17 C.F.R. § 229.308(a)(3).

The rule reads original annual reports only. HP's material weaknesses for fiscal 2022 were first
disclosed in an amended report (10-K/A, [filed 2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/47217/000004721723000075/hpq-20221031.htm)),
which the rule does not read. They were not described in terms of IT general controls, although one
concerned reliance on a third party's payment application without timely SOC 1 assurance; HP's fiscal
2022 is not a flagged firm-year, so the counts above are unchanged, but it is a second way a reported
weakness can escape the extraction.

## What follows

- The pre-validation association (β = −0.0507, p = 0.001, ten treated firm-years) is withdrawn as
  evidence about IT control failure. The regression cannot be re-estimated here: the ten treated
  firm-years are a subset of the 21 that only the frozen panel identifies.
- Next step, as a new pipeline version: classify the conclusion of Item 9A and the auditor's opinion
  (including "did not maintain effective" and "has not maintained effective"), separate current,
  remediated and historical weaknesses, match cues as whole words, read 10-K/A filings, re-estimate on
  the genuine events with wild-bootstrap inference for few treated clusters.
- To resolve the 13 open firm-years from the cached filings, run
  `04_protocol/validation/print_itgc_contexts.py` (prints every passage the rule affirmed, with its
  cue and context).
